Atari TOS images
----------------
⚠ `roms/tos102uk.img` and `roms/tos162uk.img`, when present in this package, are
**original Atari TOS ROMs**. They are NOT free software and NOT covered by the GPL:
they remain the property of their rights holders and are included only for the ST /
STE machine profiles. NeoST does not need them — it boots EmuTOS out of the box.

Since 2026-08-30 packages are built WITHOUT them by default (the repository no longer
tracks any Atari TOS). A maintainer can re-include local copies for a personal build
with `NEOST_PACKAGE_NO_ATARI_TOS=0` (see `packaging/stage_free_data.sh`).

Demoscene productions (`disks/`)
--------------------------------
Three Atari ST demos ship with NeoST so that the package can show what the emulator
does without any copyrighted game. They are **not** part of NeoST and are **not**
covered by the GPL — each remains the work and the property of its authors:

| File                  | Production          | Authors            | Year |
|-----------------------|---------------------|--------------------|------|
| `cuddly_demos.msa`    | The Cuddly Demos    | The Carebears (TCB)| 1989 |
| `nocooper.msa`        | No Cooper           | 1984               | 1989 |
| `closure.msa`         | Closure             | Sync               |      |

They are included as freely-distributed demoscene works — productions released to be
copied and shown, and archived as such (Fujiology, Demozoo, pouet.net). NeoST
redistributes them unmodified and makes no claim on them. **Rights holders who would
rather not be in this package only have to say so** — open an issue at
https://github.com/habib256/neost and they will be removed from the next release.

They double as pixel-exact test material: these three are among the references that
pin NeoST's Shifter (all four borders open, med-res overscan, fine rasters).

*references* — read as sources, and run as an oracle during development. Apart from the
IPF decoder below, no part of either is redistributed in this package.

libmt32emu and the LGPL
-----------------------
The Munt MT-32/CM-32L emulator (https://github.com/munt/munt, version 2.8.3) is vendored
under `extern/mt32emu` and linked **statically** into the GUI, which renders the ST's MIDI
OUT through a Roland MT-32/CM-32L. Upstream code is UNMODIFIED; only the CMake build file
is NeoST's (see `extern/mt32emu/NEOST_VENDOR.md`). LGPL 2.1 allows static linking as long
as the recipient can rebuild against a modified library: NeoST ships its full source under
GPL 3, this copy of Munt included, which satisfies that. Its section 3 also permits the
switch to the GPL, which settles compatibility with NeoST's GPL 3.
The Roland ROMs are NOT included: the user provides them (`roms/mt32/`).

stb_image (`extern/stb/stb_image.h`) is stb_image v2.30 by Sean Barrett and contributors,
dual-licensed MIT or public domain (Unlicense): https://github.com/nothings/stb — the GUI
uses it to decode the keyboard photo.
