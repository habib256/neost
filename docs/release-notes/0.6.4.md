## NeoST 0.6.4 — a safety net before the new CPU core

The last step before 0.6.5, which will precede the switch to the POM68K 68000 core.

**Accuracy**
- MFP timers now start on their own clock edge, as the MC68901 datasheet says: Lethal Xcess on STE gets its top border back.
- The MFP elects interrupts by fixed priority, freezes its counter when stopped and counts the pulse of an AER change in event-count mode.
- The ACIA, SCC and WD1772 follow their datasheets on a few remaining points.

**Mouse**
- Drag-and-drop works in games and on the desktop; Chessmaster is now playable.

**Games**
- Lethal Xcess and Golden Axe (STE), Rick Dangerous and Pang (launched from the GEM desktop) join the replays checked against Hatari: 23 in total.
- The TOS 2.06 UK double fault on boot with an early SPACE is explained: a real race in the ROM, not an emulation defect.

**Verification**
- The CPU swap gate now replays reference screens, game replays, the whole disk collection (ST, STE, Mega STE) and Mega STE probes at 16 MHz.
- The 68000 bench now covers bus errors and double faults against the fx68k RTL.
- Four reference screens no longer depend on the boot phase.
- The collection and the replays run clean under ASan/UBSan.

**Save states**: format v59, unchanged since 0.6.3.

Packages ship with their licences (`GPL-3.0.txt`, `GPL-2.0.txt`, `THIRD-PARTY.txt`) and contain no Atari ROM: EmuTOS is the default.
