## NeoST 0.6.4

Coming next: 0.6.5.

**What's new**
- **Accuracy**: MFP timers start on their own clock edge (Lethal Xcess on STE gets its top border back); MFP, ACIA, SCC and WD1772 closer to their datasheets.
- **Mouse**: drag-and-drop works in games and on the desktop; Chessmaster is now playable.
- **Games**: Lethal Xcess, Golden Axe, Rick Dangerous and Pang join the games replayed against Hatari (23 in total).
- **Verification**: a wider regression net (reference screens, game replays, the whole disk collection, Mega STE at 16 MHz) and a 68000 bench that now covers bus errors and double faults.

**Save states**: format v59, unchanged since 0.6.3.

Packages contain no Atari ROM: EmuTOS is the default. Licences included.
